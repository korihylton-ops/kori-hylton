Motion

Pick it up off the page

↥

Hover or focus any collage element: it straightens toward 0° and lifts 2–4px. Structured-zone components (buttons, cards) never rotate — only a small press/lift on interaction. Reduced-motion users get the shadow change without the transform.

Guidelines

Do / don't

Do

  • Keep signal-red exclusive to one action per screen
  • Apply the white die-cut outline to every pasted photo or illustration
  • Keep the structured zone calm — no grain, no rotation, no tile lettering

Don't

  • Use tile lettering in running text or navigation
  • Spread bubble-pink across large surfaces
  • Randomize rotation at runtime — hand-set it per instance
