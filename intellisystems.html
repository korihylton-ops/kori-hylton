FAQ

Questions before you get a quote

How fast can this actually be set up?
Most builds go live within 1–2 weeks. Simpler setups — just lead response and booking — can be running in a matter of days. Timeline depends on how much needs connecting to your existing tools.
Do I need to already be running ads?
No. This works whether leads come from ads, your website, referrals, or Google Business Profile. If you are running ads, we'll also show you what they're actually converting into.
What if I already have a CRM?
We build primarily on GoHighLevel, but the automation and booking layer can often connect to what you're already using. We'll tell you honestly if a switch is worth it before you commit to anything.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. Setup is a fixed one-time build, and ongoing support runs month to month. You can pause or cancel with 30 days' notice — nothing locks you in.
What's included in the quote?
A specific breakdown of what needs building for your business, a fixed setup cost, and the ongoing monthly cost if you want us managing it. No generic package — it's scoped to what you actually need.
